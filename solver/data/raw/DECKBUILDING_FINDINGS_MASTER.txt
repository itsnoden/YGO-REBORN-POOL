CURRENT #1 DECK CANDIDATE

GRAVEKEEPER ROYAL TRIBUTE LOCK / CONTROL
Status: current best deck candidate after whole-pool review. Tier 0 candidate, NOT claimed
as tournament-proven Tier 0 without actual event data.

Why it moved above D-HERO Raiza/Vanity:
- Necrovalley + Gravekeeper's Commandant + Terraforming gives 9 direct access cards to
  Necrovalley before Upstart filtering.
- Royal Tribute is unrestricted at 3 in this custom banlist.

  Gravekeeper deck is constructed to minimize self-damage from the effect.
- Necrovalley directly attacks many of the strongest engines found in this pool:
  Malicious, Treeborn Frog, Disk Commander revival, Zombie recursion, Book of Life,
  Premature Burial/Call-style graveyard recursion, Spell Striker graveyard banish costs,

  on Royal Tribute.

- Royal Oppression is also unrestricted and can be used as a supplemental lock, though
  too many copies can interfere with Spy/Rite, so it should not automatically be maxed.

THIRD WHOLE-POOL POWER AUDIT — FIND SOMETHING MORE BROKEN
----------------------------------------------------------
Goal:
- Try to dethrone Gravekeeper Prison with a true FTK/OTK, infinite loop, hand wipe, summon
  lock, or other format-specific abuse using only the confirmed 2,273-card pool and saved
  Reborn banlist.

Major challengers rechecked:
A. Protector of the Sanctuary hand-wipe ideas
- Protector + Card Destruction/Disturbance looks like a complete hand wipe at first glance, but
  those draw effects cannot legally be activated while Protector is already face-up. The wipe
  only works if Protector becomes face-up after the draw effect is activated or if a mandatory
  effect such as Morphing Jar resolves while Protector is face-up. That makes the turn-1 setup
  too specific to outrank Royal Tribute.

B. Empty Jar / deck-out FTK
- Morphing Jar, Book of Taiyou, The Shallow Grave, A Feather of the Phoenix, Serial Spell,
  Reload, Magical Mallet and Card Destruction are in the pool.
- However Cyber Jar is Forbidden, Morphing Jar is Limited to 1, Book of Moon is Limited to 1,
  and several major Empty-Jar consistency cards are not in this pool. The available version is
  too fragile and too dependent on seeing the single Morphing Jar.

C. Cyber Valley / Dimension Fusion infinite draw
- Cyber Valley, Machine Duplication, Spell Economics, Dimension Fusion and Dark Magician of
  Chaos are all present. The classic infinite loop requires Dark Magician of Chaos to return
  Dimension Fusion immediately when Special Summoned. Current DMoC text does not do that; it
  retrieves a Spell only in the End Phase and is once per turn. Therefore the loop is NOT
  promoted unless YGO Reborn is directly proven to use the pre-errata DMoC text.

D. Makyura / Magical Explosion / Life Equalizer
- Magical Explosion, Life Equalizer, Reasoning, Monster Gate, Magical Stone Excavation and
  other spell-loading tools exist.
- Current Makyura allows only one Trap from hand for the turn after it is sent from the Monster
  Zone to the GY. That is not enough for a clean first-turn Life Equalizer + Magical Explosion
  kill, and the multiple-Trap setup is less consistent than Royal Tribute Prison.

E. Manticore of Darkness + Card of Safe Return Exodia
- The two-Manticore loop with Card of Safe Return is a real infinite-draw interaction if one
  Manticore is in the GY after being sent that turn and the other is face-up to alternate as
  the send cost.
- Manticore is Semi-Limited to 2 and there is no reliable direct Manticore searcher in the
  confirmed pool, so assembly consistency remains too low to beat Gravekeepers.

F. Reversal Quiz / Black Pendant
- Reversal Quiz, Black Pendant, Wall of Revealing Light, Convulsion of Nature and Archfiend's
  Oath are present. The OTK is possible in principle but requires too many individually specific
  pieces and precise LP manipulation. It is not close to the 25%+ natural Royal Tribute opening
  rate.

G. Ultimate Offering / Gadget / Cannon Soldier burn
- Ultimate Offering, all three Gadgets, Cannon Soldier, Dark Room of Nightmare and Makyura are
  present and can produce very high burn ceilings.
- A turn-1 version requires Makyura to be sent from the Monster Zone, Ultimate Offering to be
  activated from hand, Cannon Soldier/Gadget access, plus burn amplification. The opening is far
  more multi-piece than Gravekeepers and is vulnerable to bad Monster Gate hits.

H. Demise / Doom Dozer OTK
- Demise, King of Armageddon, Doom Dozer and Megamorph are present, but Advanced Ritual Art is
  absent from the confirmed pool. The remaining ritual routes require more cards from hand and
  are not consistent enough to rank #1.

I. Horus / Spell Canceller / Jinzo hard locks
- Horus LV8, Spell Canceller, Jinzo, Royal Decree, Jowgen and Vanity monsters were reviewed as
  spell/trap/special-summon lock packages. Their ceilings are high, but they require more setup
  and leave more conventional outs than Necrovalley + Royal Tribute + draw denial.

J. Wanghu / Burden / Kaiser stun
- King Tiger Wanghu, Burden of the Mighty, Kaiser Colosseum and Royal Oppression can create a
  strong monster-summon prison. This is a legitimate anti-meta shell but is a 2-3 card board lock
  that does not generate the same immediate hand advantage as Royal Tribute.

Third-audit conclusion:
- No different archetype is promoted above Gravekeepers.
- The strongest improvement found is Drop Off. It directly converts successful Royal Tribute
  openings into a next-draw denial sequence and is unrestricted by the saved Reborn list.
- At the end of this third audit the best build was GRAVEKEEPER ROYAL TRIBUTE / DROP OFF PRISON v3; this has since been SUPERSEDED by the no-side matchup-tested v4 below.
- Re-open the ranking immediately if in-game YGO Reborn text proves PRE-ERRATA Dark Magician of
  Chaos or another historically changed combo card is implemented.

FOURTH AUDIT — NO-SIDE META MATCHUP STRESS TEST — 2026-09-04
-------------------------------------------------------------
User constraint:
- NO SIDE DECK. Every matchup judgment uses only each deck's main-deck plan. No side cards are counted as answers.

Method:
- Rebuilt/considered the strongest plausible main-deck versions from the confirmed 2,273-card pool and saved Reborn banlist rather than historical tournament lists.
- Stress-tested Gravekeeper Prison against D-HERO Monarch, pure/Brain-Control Monarch, Macro Monarch, Six Samurai, Zombies/Card of Safe Return, Gadgets/Ultimate Offering, Cyber Valley/Machine Duplication, Dark World, Burn/Stall, Counter Fairy/Horus-style control, combo shells, and the Gravekeeper mirror.
- Used opening-hand Monte Carlo for Gravekeeper consistency and key opposing combo access, plus card-interaction analysis. These are matchup-model results, not literal simulator duel records.

Royal Tribute target-density reference for 5-card hands:
- 18-monster D-HERO Monarch/Zombie shell: ~96.0% chance to open at least one monster; 2.25 monsters average. Even after committing one monster, ~76.0% of hands still contain another monster, averaging ~1.29 residual monsters.
- 17-monster pure/Macro Monarch shell: ~94.9% at least one monster; 2.13 average. After committing one, ~72.0% still hold another, averaging ~1.18.
- 19-monster Six Samurai shell: ~96.9% at least one monster; 2.38 average. After committing one, ~79.6% still hold another, averaging ~1.41.
- 15-monster Gadget shell: ~91.9% at least one monster; 1.88 average.
These densities are why Royal Tribute remains high-impact even when Gravekeepers goes second after an opponent has already committed a monster.

MATCHUP CONCLUSIONS — MAIN DECK ONLY
--------------------------------------
D-HERO MONARCH — GRAVEKEEPERS FAVORED
- Necrovalley attacks Malicious, Treeborn Frog, Disk Commander/Premature/Call-style recursion, and Spell Striker-style GY costs.
- Royal Tribute attacks a monster-dense hand; DDV specifically punishes Malicious, Disk Commander, Treeborn, Card Trooper and other low-ATK engine pieces.
- Anti-Spell delays Destiny Draw, Brain Control, Soul Exchange and generic Spell outs.
- D-HERO's best route is to go first, extract value with Destiny Draw before Necrovalley lands, then establish Vanity's Fiend/Raiza or remove Necrovalley. Grand Mole/DD Warrior Lady in v4 are included as broad main-deck answers to those established threats.

PURE / BRAIN-CONTROL MONARCH — GRAVEKEEPERS SLIGHTLY FAVORED
- This is closer because the deck is less graveyard-dependent than D-HERO.
- Brain Control/Soul Exchange into Raiza or Mobius can punish Gravekeepers, especially going first.
- Royal Tribute still attacks the Monarch/tribute-fodder hand heavily, and Anti-Spell makes the Spell-based tribute package slower.

MACRO MONARCH — CLOSEST MATCHUP; APPROXIMATELY EVEN TO SLIGHT GRAVEKEEPER EDGE
- This is the hardest mainstream matchup because Necrovalley does not meaningfully shut down D.D. Survivor/banish-based resource loops.
- Macro/Fissure also reduces the value of some graveyard-centric cards.
- Gravekeepers still has the stronger immediate hand attack in Royal Tribute and retains Anti-Spell, DDV, Torrential/Mirror/Ring, plus v4 Breaker/Dust/Phoenix to interact with continuous banish cards.
- No clear reason emerged to rank Macro Monarch above Gravekeepers across the whole field.

SIX SAMURAI — GRAVEKEEPERS SLIGHTLY FAVORED, VERY DIE-ROLL SENSITIVE
- Six Samurai is the most explosive non-combo challenger. Royal Tribute is especially punishing because optimized Six Samurai lists are monster-dense.
- A deliberately aggressive Six Samurai model using roughly 12 normal Samurai starters, 3 Grandmaster, 3 Great Shogun Shien, up to 3 Double Summon, 2 ROTA and 3 Upstart reached a turn-1 Shogun-establishment line only around the low-teens percentage range (~13% in the most aggressive tested construction).
- That Shogun opening is dangerous because it can limit the Gravekeeper player to one Spell/Trap activation for the turn, preventing Necrovalley + Royal Tribute from both being activated immediately.
- Kamon can also attack face-up Spell/Trap cards when another Six Samurai is present.
- However going first, Gravekeepers can strip Grandmaster/Shogun/Samurai from hand before the swarm happens; DDV hits many smaller Samurai and Bottomless/Oppression/Torrential punish Grandmaster/Shogun summons.

ZOMBIES / CARD OF SAFE RETURN — GRAVEKEEPERS STRONGLY FAVORED
- Necrovalley directly attacks Book of Life, Vampire Lord recursion and other graveyard-return engines.
- Royal Tribute and DDV pressure Pyramid Turtle/smaller monsters and Anti-Spell delays Safe Return/Book of Life.

GADGETS / ULTIMATE OFFERING — GRAVEKEEPERS STRONGLY FAVORED
- Necrovalley is not the key; DDV is. All three Gadgets fall in the low-ATK range and DDV can punish field/hand and future draws.
- Royal Tribute strips Gadget-heavy hands; Torrential/Mirror/Ring and Royal Oppression/control cards cover Offering-driven boards.

CYBER VALLEY / MACHINE DUPLICATION — GRAVEKEEPERS FAVORED
- Royal Tribute attacks the required monsters; DDV hits Cyber Valley/Card Trooper-type Duplication targets.
- Anti-Spell delays Machine Duplication and Royal Oppression/Torrential/Bottomless punish the explosive Special Summon turns.
- The current-text DMoC assumption prevents the previously investigated Dimension Fusion infinite loop.

DARK WORLD — CLOSE BUT STILL SLIGHT GRAVEKEEPER EDGE
- This is the natural Royal Tribute anti-synergy matchup: Broww discarded by an opponent's card effect draws 2, so a Royal Tribute can refill the Dark World player's hand instead of cleanly deleting it.
- However Necrovalley negates effects that would move cards out of the GY, suppressing Goldd/Sillva/Beiige self-Special-Summon effects under current card text.
- DDV hits Broww/Scarr and Anti-Spell delays Dark World Dealings/Lightning. Dark World is therefore a real concern, but not enough to overturn the #1 ranking.

BURN / STALL — APPROXIMATELY EVEN TO SLIGHT GRAVEKEEPER EDGE WITH v4
- This was a weakness of the 9-monster v3 because Royal Tribute/Necrovalley are less impressive versus low-monster burn.
- v4 improves the matchup without sideboarding by adding Breaker and Dust Tornado while retaining Heavy/MST/Phoenix. Anti-Spell slows Wave-Motion Cannon and other Spells; DDV punishes Des Koala/Stealth Bird-type low-ATK monsters.
- The format's saved banlist also forbids Messenger of Peace, Level Limit - Area B and Gravity Bind, removing several premier stall pieces.

COUNTER FAIRY / HORUS / GENERIC LOCK CONTROL — GRAVEKEEPERS SLIGHTLY FAVORED OVERALL
- These can produce difficult established boards and are less graveyard-dependent.
- They are slower/more multi-piece than Royal Tribute and generally expose either monsters to Tribute/DDV or continuous Spell/Trap cards to the v4 generic removal package.

GRAVEKEEPER MIRROR — EVEN
- Naturally close to 50/50 and sequencing/die-roll dependent. It does not provide a reason to rank another archetype above Gravekeepers.

Corrections found:
1. Gravekeeper's Commandant is EARTH (1600 ATK), NOT DARK. Under Necrovalley it reaches
   2100 ATK, but it still cannot satisfy Deck Devastation Virus because DDV requires a DARK
   monster with 2000 or more ATK.
2. Gravekeeper's Assailant is DARK 1500 ATK and becomes 2000 ATK under Necrovalley, so it
   is the natural direct DDV tribute in this main deck. Spy can later Special Summon Assailant.
3. Therefore the prior 2-DDV v4 ratio overestimated live tribute access. A 300,000-hand check
   found 2 DDV appeared in about 25.4% of post-Upstart opening hands, but only about 19% of
   those DDV-seen hands also had an immediate Necrovalley + Assailant line.
4. V5 correction: -1 Deck Devastation Virus, +1 Bottomless Trap Hole. This keeps one DDV's
   format-warping upside while making the second trap slot universally live against the hardest
   no-side matchups (Macro Monarch, Six Samurai, pure Monarch, Vanity's Fiend, Cyber Dragon,
   Great Shogun Shien, etc.). Bottomless removes the summoned body but does NOT negate Monarch
   summon-trigger effects; this distinction is important.
5. Royal Tribute self-discard numbers were re-run with stricter sequencing. The correct v5
   5-card-opening estimates after resolving Upstarts are approximately:
   - Necrovalley + Royal Tribute access: 25.8%
   - no monster after Upstart cycling: 12.3%
   - conditional zero own-monster discard in lock openings: 68.8%
   - average own monsters discarded in those lock openings: 0.36
   Earlier ~79.8% zero-discard / ~0.22 average figures are superseded.
6. A Terraforming ratio check still favors 3 for this deck's identity: going from 3 to 2 drops
   the natural Royal Tribute lock rate by roughly 1.8 percentage points; no tested generic
   replacement produced a clearer all-meta gain.
