## Current learning-design hypothesis to test

The current terminal REINFORCE update uses `scale = 1/sqrt(number_of_policy_steps)` and then applies that scale to every decision in the episode. Therefore the total possible gradient magnitude still increases roughly with `sqrt(episode length)`, allowing very long / chain-heavy games to exert more total training influence than short games.

Next clean experiment: compare the current normalization against **equal total credit per duel** (e.g. scale proportional to `1/number_of_policy_steps`) while holding candidate decks, seeds, policy features, legal actions, learning rate, and evaluation protocol fixed. This is a learning/credit-assignment change only; it must add no human card/deck heuristics.

Do not replace the canonical learner unless the controlled held-out A/B improves generalization.
