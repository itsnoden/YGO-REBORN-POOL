## New pilot experiments now active

Because:
- more training alone gave only +3.125 points,
- Chain Context failed replication,
- Scalar Context reversed on disjoint replication,
- earlier counterfactual and 1/N credit schemes were already rejected,

the next experiments target two narrower hypotheses.

### Phase-Turn Context A/B

New policy:
- applies phase + turn-side conditioning across all decision families;
- omits LP/hand/field/GY interactions that may have caused Scalar Context to overfit;
- adds no external strategy priors.

Workflow: `Phase Turn Context AB`.

### Discounted Terminal Credit A/B

New policy:
- retains canonical 1/sqrt(N) episode normalization;
- discounts terminal credit by distance from duel end;
- half-life: 256 learned decisions;
- aims to reduce noisy credit to very early actions in long, chain-heavy duels;
- adds no strategic labels or human heuristics.

Workflow: `Discounted Terminal AB`.
