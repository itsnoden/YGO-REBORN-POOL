## Critical duel-evaluation correction

Affected function: `solver/reborn/pilot_eval.py::run_eval_game`.

Old behavior:
- received a trained treatment policy object;
- copied its weights;
- always constructed `SparsePolicy(...)` for held-out evaluation;
- therefore any subclass-only feature generator (for example `ChainContextPolicy`) disappeared at evaluation time.

Why this matters:
- training used the treatment representation;
- held-out evaluation did **not** use that representation;
- contextual treatment weights were mostly unreachable during evaluation;
- prior chain-context A/B and replication numbers do not test the intended treatment and must not be used to decide whether chain context works.

Fix:
- frozen evaluation now clones `frozen_policy.__class__`;
- feature-generator class, temperature and weights are preserved;
- learning rate remains zero;
- new helper: `frozen_policy_copy`;
- regression test: `solver/tests/test_pilot_eval_policy_class.py`.

## Head-to-head duel infrastructure

New module:
`solver/reborn/payoff_matrix_eval.py`

Capabilities:
- trains one universal zero-prior frozen pilot on a separate training cohort;
- controls **both players** with frozen copies of the exact same policy class/weights;
- evaluates every matchup in both seat orientations on the same seeds;
- keeps information-safe observation checks on every decision;
- records wins/losses/draws separately for first/second seat;
- marks samples uncertified if there are unsupported interactions, timeouts or fallback decisions;
- builds a complete payoff matrix suitable for the existing conservative maximin/double-oracle layer.

Important: the module intentionally reports `deck_ranking_evidence: false` until a pilot-skill treatment is actually promoted. Infrastructure existence is not permission to declare a #1 deck.
